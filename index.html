30 minutes by flying car vs driving

How much more land you reach in 30 minutes by flying car than by driving. Pick a city, or step through the four views. 25 US cities.

What is real: 30-minute drive area from OpenStreetMap routing (Valhalla public server, no live traffic). Flight circles at 120 and 250 mph, straight line, no takeoff or wind. Terrain dots from AWS Terrarium elevation tiles. Car times from OSRM. Lakes show as land, so some multipliers are inflated. Destination coordinates are approximate. Garages are not shown. Fan rebuild of an idea in a post by Tsung Xu. Not affiliated with Vight Aero.
